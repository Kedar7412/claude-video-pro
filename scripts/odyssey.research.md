# Research: the journey of Odysseus (2026-11-01)

## Facts (used in script)
Source A: Wikipedia, Odyssey (plot summary) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odyssey
- ~10 years of the Trojan War + ~10 years journeying home = ~20 years away.
- Lotus-eaters' fruit made the crew forget about going home.
- Odysseus told Polyphemus his name was "Nobody", blinded him; then revealed his real name, and Polyphemus asked his
  father Poseidon for revenge.
- Aeolus' bag of winds: the crew opened it and were blown off course just as they nearly reached Ithaca.
- Laestrygonians destroyed all but one of the ships.
- Circe: Odysseus stayed with her for a year.
- Sirens: crew plugged ears with beeswax; Odysseus tied to the mast.
- Crew ate Helios' sacred cattle; Zeus destroyed the last ship; Odysseus sole survivor.
- Argos, the old dog, recognised Odysseus and then died.
- 108 suitors. 24 books, 12,109 lines; composed c. 8th century BC.

Source B: study guides
- Polyphemus: other Cyclopes ignore him because "Nobody" is hurting him; Poseidon makes the journey long.
  https://www.brighthubeducation.com/homework-help-literature/37947-summary-of-the-odyssey-including-cyclops-cicones-sirens-sylla

  Same Bright Hub page; Shmoop Book 12 https://www.shmoop.com/study-guides/odyssey/book-xii-summary.html

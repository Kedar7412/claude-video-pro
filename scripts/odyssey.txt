Saying his own name cost him *10* years.

This is Odysseus, king of the island of *Ithaca*.

He's just spent 10 years fighting at *Troy*.

All he wants now is to sail *home*.

First, an island whose fruit makes you *forget*.

His men eat it and stop caring about *home*.

He has to drag them back to the *ships*.

Next, a cave, and a one-eyed giant: *Polyphemus*.

Odysseus tells the giant his name is *Nobody*.

They blind him, and he screams that Nobody is *hurting* him.

So no other giant comes to *help*.

But sailing away, Odysseus shouts his *real* name.

The giant's father is Poseidon, god of the *sea*.

And Poseidon makes his trip home *brutal*.

Would you have kept *quiet*? Yes or no?

The wind god gives him a bag full of *winds*.

Home is almost in sight, and his crew opens the *bag*.

The winds blow them far from *Ithaca*.

Then giant cannibals destroy every ship but *one*.

The witch Circe turns his men into *pigs*.

He wins them back, and stays with her a full *year*.

Then the Sirens, singing sailors to their *death*.

His crew plugs their ears with *beeswax*.

He has himself tied to the *mast*, just to hear it.

Pick one: six-headed monster, or ship-eating *whirlpool*?

He picks the monster, Scylla. She takes *6* men.

Starving, the crew eats the sun god's sacred *cattle*.

Zeus destroys the ship. Only Odysseus *survives*.

He washes up on Calypso's island and stays *7* years.

She offers him *immortality*. He still chooses home.

When he finally reaches Ithaca, *20* years have passed.

*108* suitors fill his house, chasing his wife, Penelope.

Only his old dog Argos *recognizes* him.

Penelope's test: string his bow, shoot through *12* axes.

Every suitor fails. Odysseus *doesn't*.

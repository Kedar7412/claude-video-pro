Bleeding gums when you brush aren't *normal*.

*47%* of US adults over 30 have gum disease.

Brushing can't *reach* tartar below the gumline.

*Scaling* scrapes the plaque and tartar off your roots.

Root *planing* smooths those roots so tartar can't reattach.

Expect numbing, and often more than one *visit*.

Mild soreness usually fades within a few *days*.

Gums bleed when you brush? Ask your dentist about a *deep* cleaning.
